# Fright House — top-down campaign

Play the approved first level at http://localhost:8089/two-d/index.html.

Level 1 includes the street/facade, animated carnival bulbs, turning Ferris wheel, drifting fog, a newspaper on the bench, missing-child notices, admission ticket, shuttered stall and working entrance turnstile. E / EXAMINE interacts; arrows/WASD move; click or tap the path to walk; F toggles light; J / NOTES opens the journal. Pause freezes animation and sound. Continue Investigation restores locally saved notes and position; Restart starts a new investigation.

The remaining 19 campaign areas are planned, not playable yet. Each receives a concept review before implementation. Character appearance is temporary pending the user's explicit design approval.

The original playable ticket-hall study is preserved at http://localhost:8089/two-d/ticket-hall.html. It remains a visual/movement comparison, not the final Level 2 campaign implementation.

See IMPLEMENTATION.md for the accepted direction, art approval, source prompts and validation. The 3D game is unchanged.
