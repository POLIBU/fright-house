From the bones beneath the funhouse to a desperate ride through its maze. These are the final two levels. The approved concept is shown below.

10 · The Bone Gallery

The landing dust clears. Children's clothes cover the floor; varied scarecrows turn toward you from the walls, and ceiling drips leave wet patches. Frightened, the detective runs through the gallery to the waiting cart.

Separate 3D bone props, walls and cart, with a clear walking route and easy boarding. The camera follows the escape.

11 · The Last Ride

Stay in the cart and choose turns through a branching maze. A huge spider made from human arms and legs crawls behind you. Wrong turns cost distance; find the real exit before it catches you.

Queue turns before each junction. Loops and dead ends allow turnarounds. Reach the outside to finish the game; retry from the cart if caught.

The sunrise escape

Walk out through the side gate, enter your car and drive away as dawn breaks.

Playable: four 3D bone heaps, eight turning scarecrows, nineteen reactive 3D clothes, a 3D cart, lateral obstacle dodging, buffered maze turns and twelve spider animation frames.
