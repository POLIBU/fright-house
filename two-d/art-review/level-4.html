The forgotten toy storeroom

The upper exit leads into a quiet stockroom. The machinery dies behind you. Something moves inside the prize cage.

Approved sequence

  1. Open the storeroom door to reveal the room beyond it.
  2. Explore around the solid shelves, workbench and central prize cage. Read the child’s drawing for a warning.
  3. Inspect the wind-up rabbit in real 3D and turn its key three times.
  4. The lights fade and switch off. Twenty-four toys hop down and come alive. Keep moving around the cage while the red door’s bolt releases.
  5. Open the red door and escape through it.

Playable version

The approved painting remains the visual foundation. Toys and doors are separate procedural 3D objects; furniture uses solid collision and painted relief layers. The wind-up toy has a live 3D close-up. Pause and retry reset the sequence correctly.
