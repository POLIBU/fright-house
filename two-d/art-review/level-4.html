LEVEL 4 · CONCEPT FOR REVIEW · NOT YET PLAYABLE

The forgotten toy storeroom

The upper exit leads into a quiet stockroom. The machinery dies behind you. Something moves inside the prize cage.

Proposed sequence

  1. Enter from the upper landing and open the storeroom door. Its interior is revealed when the door opens.
  2. A brief burst of flies and crawling spiders comes from the foul alcove. Explore the paths around the solid shelves and prize cage.
  3. Inspect the damaged toys and collect a child's drawing. The drawing records three symbols the children saw on the forbidden door.
  4. Find the winding key on the cage. Examine the music box in real 3D, turn its three dials to match the drawing, then wind it.
  5. The red door unlocks as a toy turns its head. Take the drawing onward; a checkpoint preserves the discovery.

Build details after review

Keep the illustrated appearance. Model the cage, cabinets and music box as separate procedural 3D layers with collision footprints, rather than letting the player walk across painted objects. Give the music box a live 3D inspection and animated lid, dials and winding key. Use the existing pause, journal and touch controls.

This is the proposed Level 4 layout and gameplay, not a completed level. Level 3 remains playable. Following your earlier direction, each new level's concept is shown for feedback before production.
