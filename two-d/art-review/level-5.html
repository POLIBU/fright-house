The specimen library

A silent office above the attraction. A telephone rings beside a green banker’s lamp.

Playable sequence

  1. Enter beside the heavy wooden desk. Its telephone rings until you lift the receiver in a real 3D close-up.
  2. Children’s voices whisper: “You can’t escape.”
  3. Approach the specimen shelves. A jar tips, falls and breaks.
  4. A pale worm with a human face crawls out, raises its head and spits bile toward your last position. Escape through the right-hand opening into a long scrolling library. Weave around shelves to the far-end exit, then work its latch while avoiding the creature.

Separate 3D props

The desk, lamp, phone, shelves and jars are separate procedural Three.js objects, with collision and depth ordering. The lamp casts a warm yellow-green pool over the desk and worn patterned carpet. The approved office now leads to 3.5 screens of library. The camera follows a larger investigator. The exit lock needs 2.4 seconds to release.
