Proposed play sequence

  1. Enter from the library and hear the barrel mechanism starting.
  2. Watch the warning light and time your movement past red and blue steel paint drums, using clear side recesses to dodge. A hit releases paint rather than fire.
  3. Reach the gate mechanism, inspect and turn its handwheel, then escape up the stairs.

For production after your review: barrels, rack, gate and wheel become separate 3D layers with connected geometry; solid bases and clear space behind props determine walking routes. Recesses must remain usable, with clutter moved out of the safe area.
