The Animatronic Workshop

Play the boss fight →

Enter from below into darkness. The operating lamp reveals a giant clown lying on its platform. Collect the metal fork, evade its grasp, and short the contacts when its chest opens.

  1. The clown wakes, walks and lunges. Hit its casing twice to force the contacts open, then strike again to short it.
  2. At half health its damaged body fires lightning in all directions at unpredictable intervals.
  3. Defeat it to release the upper-right exit. Moths circle the lamps; footprints fade in the dusty floor.

The platform is procedural Three.js geometry. The clown and detective use separate transparent animation frames.
