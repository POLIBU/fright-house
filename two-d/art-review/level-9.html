LEVEL 9 · APPROVED AND PLAYABLE

The Fall

PLAY LEVEL 9 →

Beyond the defeated animatronic, an unlocked door leads into a dark pit.

01 · Through the door

Start on a boarded floor. Open the exit and walk through it, then spend one second in complete darkness before the camera follows the detective down.

02 · Steer through the shaft

Move left and right during the fall to avoid solid beams, broken platforms, hanging weights and pipes. Obstacles use separate procedural Three.js geometry, with the same pixelated finish as the other levels.

03 · Bones and dust

Land on a pile of dry bones. Bones scatter slightly and a heavy dust cloud rolls outward before slowly clearing.

The camera scrolls vertically with the fall. No attack-warning overlays. The playable version has a hinged 3D door, eleven moving 3D obstacles, twelve falling/landing sprite frames in the original detective style, and a procedural bone pile with a dust burst.
